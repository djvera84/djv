About

I'm Managing Director and Head of Machine Learning, AI and Quantitative Research at TWG Global AI. My team builds the data pipelines, models and production dashboards behind work for clients in asset management, consumer finance and prediction markets.

I started in pure mathematics. I finished my B.S. at UC Irvine at 17 and my Ph.D. at MIT at 21, working in algebraic topology with Lars Hesselholt. I then spent twelve years at Surlamer Investments, eventually leading quantitative research for a long-short equity derivatives fund. At Tala I built credit models that made instant loan decisions for people without credit histories, and at Capital One I built the portfolio simulator and customer lifetime value models that credit managers use to set policy.

Across all of it, I care about the whole path from data to decision: getting the data right, building a model that holds up out of sample, and putting it into a system people actually use.
